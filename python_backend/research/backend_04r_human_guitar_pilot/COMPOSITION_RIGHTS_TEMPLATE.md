# Composition rights template

Status: UNSIGNED TEMPLATE. NEEDS_HUMAN_APPROVAL.

Complete one form for each composition used in the pilot, or one form that
lists every take if a single rights holder covers all of them. A blank form
is not a grant. Do not invent a rights holder. This file is not legal
advice. Recording a composition is not authorized until GATE-4 is granted
for that composition.

If the answer to both questions is no, the take is out of the pilot.

## Rules

- The pilot uses original musical material, or a pattern the rights holder
  can show is in the public domain.
- The rights holder is the person or entity named above, or the public
  domain. "The person who happened to record the file" is not automatically
  the rights holder.
- Unauthorized commercial covers are excluded. That includes popular songs,
  transcriptions of commercial records, and Guitar Pro files of works the
  project does not have rights to.
- GuitarSet excerpts, and any other copyrighted lead sheet already used in
  the sealed baseline, are not replayed for this pilot.
- A performance that departs into an existing copyrighted song is stopped
  and the take is rejected.

## Recording-use authorization

If the rights holder signs, the project may record the named takes and use
the guitar audio for the research pilot described in `PILOT_OVERVIEW.md`:
human annotation, storage under the approved access list, and a later
research comparison that does not receive the click or the score.

This authorization is not a transfer of the composition copyright.

## Optional redistribution grant

The rights holder marks exactly one line. Neither line is pre-selected.

- [ ] Redistribution declined. The composition, as recorded, is not
  published.
- [ ] Research redistribution granted, under this license:
  ____________________.

If the performer consent form declines redistribution, this grant does not
override it. Both the performer and the rights holder have to allow
publication before any file is published. When they are the same person,
they still complete both forms so the two permissions stay separate.
